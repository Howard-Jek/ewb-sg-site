    ENGINEERS
    WITHOUT BORDERS

    Everyone has the power to change the world

    Engineers Without Borders (EWB) is a non-profit organization which seeks to empower and improve the lives of disadvantaged communities around the world.

    The Singapore chapter, which started in 2012, sets out to bring sustainable and impactful engineering solutions to communities in need via education,

    clean energy and water, and sanitation and hygiene (WASH).

    OUR STORY

    We believe that every person has the right to live in a safe and habitable environment with access to education, clean water and sanitation services.

     

    Our goal is simple.

    We want to help people around the world achieve this.

      

    EWB Singapore is made up of volunteers from all walks of life – students studying engineering, management or business; professionals working in the private sector; civil servants working in government agencies; retirees; even young children!

     

    We all share one common passion.

    We want to make a difference by building a more inclusive future for all,

    one project at a time

    Our Story

    VISION

    To create a world where every person has the right to live in a safe and habitable environment

    MISSION

    To bring sustainable and impactful engineering solutions to communities in need

    AIM

    To gather those who want to make a difference by empowering and equipping them to deliver these solutions to communities in need
